Kyoto alleyblockout

京都の路地
A01Art作品

Walked 先斗町 Pontochō on Street View for an hour. What makes these alleys read as Kyoto isn't the wood — it's the 暖簾 noren in the doorways and the lanterns at eye level.

## Reference hunt

  • 提灯 chōchin: emission ≈ 4, warm orange, never quite centred
  • 室外機 AC units on every wall. There is no such thing as too many
  • 電線 wires. So many wires. Curve + array modifier

## Next

**Roof overhangs** are too short. Push them out 30 cm and the alley gets as dark as it should
